Our focus areas

Applied research for a resilient energy transition

We combine engineering, data and territorial knowledge to design initiatives that can be measured, validated and scaled.

Energy and sustainable transition

Renewable energy, efficiency, hydrogen, storage, hybrid systems and technical performance verification.

Bioeconomy and circular economy

Organic-resource recovery, biomass, bioinputs, dark fermentation, waste traceability and circular systems.

Territorial intelligence and technology

GIS, data science, modelling, sensors, automation and responsible artificial intelligence for better decisions.

Sustainable infrastructure and habitats

Integrated solutions for housing and communities across energy, water, food, materials and green infrastructure.

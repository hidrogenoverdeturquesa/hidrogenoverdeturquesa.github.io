Hydrogen as a lifestyle may be the closest thing to Solarpunk

June 10, 2025 By Oscar Torres

Solarpunk is not just an aesthetic of solar panels and gardens on walls or roofs. This concept, although utopian, seeks a way to cohabit on this planet. For us, the incorporation of hydrogen makes us reflect on the fact that everything that moves us is energy. Communities must ally or seek that alliance to be able to store energy in the form of hydrogen and take advantage of it in both ways: in electricity or in calorific value.

Logistics as a fundamental pillar to promote operations in different organizations

June 8, 2025 By Jhon Avella

Logistics is a strategic pillar that guarantees the efficiency of operations in companies, their correct Planning and management allow you to optimize resources, reduce costs and improve response times, ensuring that each process—from supply to final delivery—is executed in a coordinated and fluid manner, In an increasingly competitive business environment, logistics not only drives productivity, but It also strengthens customer satisfaction and the ability to adapt to more sustainable market changes.

Sustainable Supply Chains: The Power of Renewable Energy

June 5, 2025 By Lizeth Torres

Decarbonizing supply chains is no longer an option, but a global necessity. One of the fundamental pillars to achieve this is the integration of renewable energies in each link in the process, from production to distribution. Unlike fuel fossils, clean energies such as solar, wind and green hydrogen, not only reduce emissions, but they also improve operational efficiency and reduce dependence on volatile markets, therefore they must be audited energy consumption in each phase of the chain. Large corporations are already migrating to solar or wind farms to supply their production centers, while freight transportation begins to be electrified with fleets powered by batteries or hydrogen. However, the real challenge is scalability: renewable technologies must be accessible for companies of all sizes and adapt to the geographical and logistical particularities of each sector.
